# Dog Boba Production Specification

## Identity
ID: `dog_boba`. Friendly, energetic, playful. Bouncy movement, expressive ears, tail and paws. Audio: friendly short vocalizations, playful bark/whine/sniff/pant where appropriate.

## Visual audit
Silhouette, body proportions, eyes, ears, mouth, paws, tail, optional collar/accessory. Ensure materials, rig and facial capability meet art direction.

## Animation targets
Idle breathing, look around, tail wag, ear reaction, head tilt, blink; pet interaction, jump, paw, pickup/release; canonical emotion clips; sleep enter/loop/wakeup.

## Audio identity
Short, friendly and energetic but restrained. Avoid repetitive barking, loudness and spam. Event variants for happy, excited, petting, interaction, wakeup and rare idle. Sleeping ambience optional and default conservative.

## Dependencies
Begin production after Mochi validates the pipeline, controller interfaces, manifest and QA standards.
