# Bunny Cloud Production Specification

## Identity
ID: `bunny_cloud`. Gentle, calm, shy; slow graceful motion; soft pastel visual identity; gentle squeak/sniff vocalization.

## Locked identity (`species.js`)
Snow-white bunny, **long ears with peach tips**, pink nose, puff tail, small green sprout on the head, strong blush. (Manifest label `netherland_dwarf` is a short-eared breed — fix in CP-10; the long ears are the identity.)

## Visual audit
Silhouette, long ears, ear rig, gentle eyes, palette, soft body, paws and optional cheek/accessory detail. Maintain readability and material quality.

## Animation targets
Breathing, ear sway/reaction, blink/slow blink, head tilt, look around, pet, nuzzle, hop, pickup/release; canonical emotions; sleep enter/loop/wakeup.

## Audio identity
Very soft, low-volume, short, sparse squeaks and gentle vocalizations. Avoid excessive repetition. Sleeping ambience optional and normally disabled.

## Dependencies
Reuse production framework established by Mochi. Validate model, rig, animation, audio and policy in the actual overlay.
