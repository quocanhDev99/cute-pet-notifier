# Penguin Pip Production Specification

## Identity
ID: `penguin_pip`. Quirky, curious and expressive; compact silhouette; wobble and playful short movements; chirps and short trills.

## Locked identity (`species.js`)
Pastel slate-blue body with a white belly and face mask, orange beak and feet, flippers, yellow knit beanie. Not charcoal/black.

## Visual audit
Body silhouette, black/white separation, beak, feet, eye expression, belly material, wing rig and optional scarf. Avoid overloading accessories.

## Animation targets
Breathing, wobble, look around, head tilt, wing motion, blink; pet, waddle, hop, spin (only if safe and comfortable visually), pickup/release; canonical emotions; sleep enter/loop/wakeup.

## Audio identity
Quirky short chirps, trills, petting reactions, happy/excited/surprised/thinking (optional), rare idle and wakeup. Keep sound distinctive but sparse.

## Dependencies
Reuse Mochi production standards and shared runtime. Confirm performance, transition quality and manifest validation.
